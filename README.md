**Ban Information & Appeal Guide**

This page provides comprehensive details about Roblox and Discord bans, including appeal procedures and Blacktag rules.

---

### Roblox Ban Information

**When Bans Are Issued**  
Bans issued in-game are initially processed and reviewed within the first 24 hours. During this period, you can submit an appeal.

**Appeal Window**  
- You may submit an appeal **after 24 hours** have passed since the ban was issued.  
- This waiting period allows staff to review evidence and gather necessary context.

**Blacktag Bans (Roblox)**  
Certain violations result in a Blacktag Ban, which is logged for safety and moderation purposes.  
Common reasons include:  
- Exploiting or attempting to modify the game  
- Doxxing or sharing private information  

*Note:* Blacktag bans are typically permanent.

**Roblox Ban Appeal Form**  
[Click here to appeal](https://tally.so/r/RGGZ89)  
*Appeals are accepted only after the 24-hour waiting period.*

### Discord Ban Information

**Types of Bans**  
- **Temporary Bans:** For minor disruptions, appealable after 48 hours.  
- **Permanent Bans:** For serious violations or repeated offenses, may be appealed once with valid reasoning.  
- **Severe Bans:** Severe misconduct can result in a Blacktag Ban across all platforms.

**Appeal Rules**  
- Temporary bans: can be appealed **after 48 hours**.  
- Permanent bans: can be appealed **once** with a valid reason.  
- Ban evasion: results in the loss of appeal rights.

**Severe & Blacktag Bans (Discord)**  
Severe violations include:  
- Harassment or hate speech  
- Threats or malicious intent  
- Doxxing or sharing harmful links  

**Discord Ban Appeal Form**  
[Click here to appeal](https://tally.so/r/WOOePe)  
*Remember to follow the specified timeframes before submitting an appeal.*

---

### Final Notes

- All appeals are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.  
- Submission of an appeal does not guarantee the removal of a ban.
